The Origin

Where It
All Began

"We didn't build Iron Peak to be another gym. We built it to be the place you think about at 5AM when everything in you says stay in bed."

It started in 2013 with a single room, second-hand barbells, and an unshakeable belief that training could be transformational - not just physical. Two coaches, Marcus Cole and Leon Voss, opened the doors of what was then a 1,200 sq ft warehouse on the edge of the city.

What they didn't expect was the community that would form. People who'd never set foot in a gym found themselves coming back every day. Not because they had to - but because Iron Peak felt like home.

Today we operate across 3 flagship locations, coach over 2,400 active members, and run the most comprehensive fitness program in the region. But the philosophy hasn't changed: push harder, recover smarter, and never stop becoming.

The Journey

How We Got Here

Every legend has a beginning. Here's ours.

2013

The First Iron

Marcus and Leon open a 1,200 sq ft warehouse gym with second-hand equipment and an unshakeable vision. First 40 members join in week one.

2015

First Expansion

Tripled our floor space, added dedicated yoga and functional training studios. Membership surpasses 300. Aria Sharma joins as Head Yoga Coach.

2017

The CrossFit Era

Launched our CrossFit program under Coach Zara Knox. First Iron Peak member competes in the Regional CrossFit Games. Community explodes to 800 members.

2019

Recovery Suite Opens

Invested in our first luxury recovery suite - cold plunge, infrared sauna, cryotherapy. Iron Peak becomes the first gym in the city with a full recovery offering.

2021

Through the Storm

Navigated the pandemic with virtual coaching, outdoor sessions, and community support. Not a single member lost. Emerged stronger with 1,500 active members.

2025

The Peak

Three flagship locations. 2,400+ members. 28 elite trainers. AI-powered progress tracking. And just getting started.
